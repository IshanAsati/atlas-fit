# Training-first redesign direction

Development-only surface contract. Applies to `src/App.tsx` and the Today, Plan,
Library, workout, Progress, and Settings surfaces. Mode: Operate.

## THESIS

Help a trainee begin an appropriate session and record actual performance with
minimal friction. Refuse the decorative dashboard that hides the workout behind
metrics and silently creates session records.

## OWN-WORLD

Matte forest `#101916`, forest surfaces `#1B2923`, chalk text `#F3F6EF`, muted
sage `#A6B4AA`, soft lime actions `#B9E27C`, and amber warnings `#F2BC67`.
Rounded 10–16px controls, restrained separation, Source Sans 3 headings and
native body/control typography. Rose `#E86672` and lavender `#A88BDA` encode
primary and secondary muscles independently of brand actions.

## STORY

Today offers the next workout. Plan previews adaptations and manages reusable
routines. Library explains movements; Progress shows recorded history, strength,
and muscle involvement. Explicit session starts lead to focused logging; an
unfinished workout is protected, and completion saves actual—not invented—sets.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

Today presents greeting, real exercise previews, and Start/Resume before weekly
activity. At 390×844, training shows compact session controls, exercise context,
and editable load/reps/Done controls above the fixed toolbar. Demonstrations
expand on request; set options reveal warm-up type, copying, and effort.

## FORM

Code-led, iPhone-first personal training companion, using original rounded,
faceless, individually selectable front/back muscle artwork. The user authorised
the redesign and implementation; no concept seed, QUALITY BAR card, or approved
decision comp exists in this task. None is invented retroactively.

## FINISH

Unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish
review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
Chromium verification is distinguished from actual iPhone Safari and VoiceOver
acceptance. Existing RepDB illustrations retain their attribution/licence and
are not restyled. New app-icon rasters embed their programmatic origin.
